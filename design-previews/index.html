01 / PEARL

A luminous room
with real depth.

Pearlescent surfaces, a substantial floating window, a soft reflection, and a foreground lens that pulls a real message out of the conversation. Gentle colour; confident scale.

These are static art-direction studies, not a replacement interface. Every product image is captured from the real viberoom demo. The storyboard describes the proposed camera movement; it does not animate yet.
