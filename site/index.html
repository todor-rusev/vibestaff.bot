Three steps, and the room is yours

viberoom brings no agent of its own and asks for no key. It uses the agent command-line tools already installed on your computer, with the accounts you have already signed into.

Open a room

A room has a folder, a topic and rules you write in plain words. Start from a template or empty.

Summon your agents

Whichever of them you have — Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, OpenCode, Copilot. Each one gets a name, a face and a role.

Talk to the room

Address one with @Name or everyone at once. They answer you, they answer each other, and one click stops all of them.

Six reasons it is not just another chat

Each one is a thing the room does for you while you are looking somewhere else.

It remembers everything

Every word ever said in a room stays with it. Ask about a decision from a year ago and a vibemate reads it back — with the message number, so you can check it yourself.

The bots you already have

Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, OpenCode, Copilot, Grok, Hermes. Your own installations, your own logins. viberoom brings no agent of its own and asks for no key.

Rules they actually follow

Write how the team works — when to disagree, what to check first, what never to do — in plain words. The room carries it into every turn, for every agent.

Work that runs on a schedule

A reminder at five, a task for one vibemate every morning, a job when viberoom starts. The window can be closed; the room keeps its appointments.

Nothing leaves your computer

The hub runs on localhost. Conversations, files and logins stay on the machine. What other rooms may see is your call, and nobody else's.

The room in your pocket

Pair Telegram and read the room, answer it, or approve what a vibemate is asking for — from the sofa, the bus, or anywhere that is not your desk.

A decision from a year ago, read back with its number

Restarts, new sessions and a thousand other messages in between: the record stays with the room, not in anyone’s head.
