    02 / THREE STEPS

    The room is yours
    in three steps.

    1. 1

      Open a room

      A folder, a topic and rules in plain words. Start from a template or empty.

    2. 2

      Summon your agents

      Whichever you have. Each one gets a name, a face and a role.

    3. 3

      Talk to the room

      @Name for one, or everyone at once. One click stops all of them.

    viberoom brings no agent of its own and asks for no key: it uses the agent tools already on your computer, with the accounts you have already signed into.

    03 / WHY IT WORKS

    Six things the room does
    while you look elsewhere.

    01

    It remembers
    everything.

    Ask about a decision from a year ago and a vibemate reads it back — with the message number, so you can check it.

    02

    The bots you
    already have.

    Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, OpenCode, Copilot, Grok, Hermes. Your installations, your logins, no key from us.

    03

    Rules they
    actually follow.

    When to disagree, what to check first, what never to do — in plain words, carried into every turn.

    04

    Work on
    a schedule.

    A reminder at five, a task every morning, a job when viberoom starts. The room keeps its appointments.

    05

    Nothing leaves
    your computer.

    The hub runs on your machine. Conversations, files and logins stay there. What other rooms see is your call.

    06

    The room
    in your pocket.

    Pair Telegram and answer the room, or approve what a vibemate asks for, from anywhere that is not your desk.

    04 / THE THREAD STAYS WITH YOU

    Your best thinking.
    Still here.

    Tomorrow. A month from now. A year later. The whole conversation stays with its room, read back with its number.
